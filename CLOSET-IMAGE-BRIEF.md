# Closet tile images — a brief to paste elsewhere

Copy the **style block** once, then one **tile prompt** per image. Seventeen
images in total (seventeen counting the 1:1).

Save each as `public/closet/<filename>.jpg`, tell me when they're in, and
I'll wire them into the tiles.

---

## Style block — paste this at the top of every prompt

> Soft natural-light photography, shot on 50mm, shallow depth of field.
> Warm honey and pale pink palette with icy-blue accents, cream background.
> Airy, feminine, unfussy — the light of a late morning through a window.
> No text, no logos, no faces, no people looking at camera.
> Square 1:1 crop, subject slightly off-centre, generous negative space at
> the top left so a label can sit over it.
> Editorial and calm, not stocky, not glossy, not over-saturated.

## Notes

**No faces.** Every one of these works better as objects and light. Faces
date quickly, they're a casting decision, and a stranger's face on a tile
called "Your household" is odd.

**Square, and dark enough at the top left.** The label sits over the image.
If a picture comes back bright in that corner I'll add a scrim, but it looks
better if the photo already has somewhere for the words to live.

**Retreats is the one to shoot for real** if you ever can — you have Sedona
photography, and a real place beats a generated one every time. Same for
`one-to-one` if you have a picture of your own space.
